# 21–24 inch: what each size actually costs

Researched 2026-08-12. US retail, new unless marked. All prices pre-tax.

The habitual size is **21″** — used for many years, though across a three-monitor
desktop. Here that habit competes with the fact that a single panel is doing the
work of three. Both sizes are specced below so the trade is visible.

## The rule that governs this whole range

**Smaller is not cheaper.** Chassis, stand, PSU and controller board cost about the
same regardless of panel size, so the small sizes carry worse specs at similar or
higher prices. Concretely:

| Size | Cheapest with a full four-way stand |
|---|---|
| 19–19.5″ | $85–120, and mostly 1600×900 or 1440×900 |
| 21.5″ | ~$170 new, or $95–110 refurbished |
| **23.8″** | **$99 new** |

A 23.8″ HP E24 G5 at $99 is cheaper than any 21.5″ equivalent and 30% more screen.
That is not an argument against 21.5″ — it is a statement of what the preference
costs, which is roughly **$70, or nothing at all if bought refurbished**.

## 21.5″ options

| Where | [Best Buy $169.99](https://www.bestbuy.com/site/dell-21-5-ips-led-fhd-100hz-monitor-vga-usb-hdmi-black-silver-multicolor/6584763.p?skuId=6584763) (third-party seller), [Dell direct](https://www.dell.com/en-us/shop/dell-pro-22-plus-monitor-p2225h/apd/210-blxj/monitors-monitor-accessories) SKU 210-BLXJ |

Anything above about $180 for this model means a marketplace reseller rather than
Dell or Best Buy direct.

### Cheaper new: HP E22 G5

Same 150 mm four-way stand and 99% sRGB IPS, but 75 Hz and 1000:1 contrast rather
than 100 Hz and 1500:1. Sold new at [HP.com](https://www.hp.com/us-en/shop/pdp/hp-e22-g5-fhd-monitor)
and Amazon, generally under $200 — **no firm live price was confirmed on
2026-08-12**, so check before ordering. Part number 6N4E8AA#ABA. It is the 21.5″
sibling of the E24 G5 recommended at 23.8″, and like it, is an older generation.

## Which to take

**If the 21″ habit matters more than the money:** Dell **P2225H at ~$170**. It is
the better monitor of the two Dells specced here — 100 Hz and 1500:1 contrast —
and it is current production with a warranty.

**If matching the ~$99 budget matters more:** a refurbished **Dell P2222H at
$95–110**, which buys the same 150 mm four-way stand at 60 Hz.

**If neither habit nor size preference is binding:** the 23.8″ **HP E24 G5 at $99**
remains the best value in the whole range, and is the more sensible panel for a
setup where one screen replaces three.

## Model-naming traps in this range

- `P2225H` = 21.5″ 16:9 1080p · `P2425H` = 23.8″ 16:9 1080p · `P2425` = 24″ 16:10
  1920×1200 · `P2425E` = 16:10 plus USB-C 90 W PD and Ethernet
- `E22 G5` / `E24 G5` = 1080p 16:9 · `E24i` = 1920×1200 · `E24u` = adds USB-C ·
  `E24t` = touch
- `SH242Y Ebmihx` has VGA · `SH242Y Ebmihux` swaps VGA for USB-C

Read the resolution and size fields, not the model number.
